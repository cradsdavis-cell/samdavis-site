What people ask first

  • I already use ChatGPT or Claude.

    So did almost everyone I've done a roadmap with. Set up once, it knows your clients, your work and how you write, and works in your email and calendar on limits you choose.

  • Will it sound like AI?

    At first, a bit. It starts from real samples of your writing, taken when it's set up, and gets closer each time you edit a draft and show it what you changed. Nothing goes out until you've read it and said yes.

  • I'm not technical.

    You don't need to be. We do it together on screen, and if we set it up together, your first month of membership is included: answers on Slack and a weekly drop-in call.

  • Is this another subscription?

    If you already pay for Claude, nothing new: it runs on your Claude plan, plus a small server in your own account from about €6 a month. If you use ChatGPT today, a Claude plan is the one new subscription.

  • Where does my data go?

    Into accounts in your name: a small server, a private GitHub folder that holds its memory, and your Claude plan. What you type goes to Claude, on Anthropic's servers. I never hold it.

  • What if you stop doing this?

    It lives in your own accounts and keeps running without me. It's yours. How →
