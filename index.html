Free and yours

No subscription. No hosted box. Nothing central.

Crads-AI used to be a hosted product. In September 2026 that ended, on purpose: the subscriptions and the hosted boxes were retired and the whole thing became free, open source and self-hosted. There is no account with Crads-AI, no server of mine in the chain, and no way for anyone but you to read what your assistant knows.

  • Your server. Built in your own hosting account and billed to you by the host. The only key on it is your computer's.
  • Your brain repo. Everything it knows about you lives in a private GitHub repository you own, backed up there every night.
  • Your Claude. It thinks with your own Claude subscription. Anthropic bills you; nobody else does.

If you ever want out, everything you need to leave is written down, and the code is open source under the AGPL-3.0, so the assistant cannot be taken away from you.
