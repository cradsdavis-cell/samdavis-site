How to

Invite and manage members

Everything a host does with members happens on the Members page.

The two kinds of membership

Worth getting straight before you invite anyone, because it decides who pays and what happens if the arrangement ends.

The last row is the one people expect to differ, and it does not. A member keeps their mineral either way. An anchored member whose host stops paying does not lose their brain; the arrangement about hosting ends, the mineral does not.

Paying for someone's box buys them hosting. It does not buy you their data, and it does not buy you control of their mineral. The hosting agreement says so in as many words.

Inviting someone

Send them an invitation from the Members page. What they get is a link, and what that link does is open a door.

Three things to tell them up front, because they are the things that confuse people:

  • They sign in with their own account. The invitation identifies which mineral,

their sign-in decides who holds it.

  • It has to be the address you invited. A forwarded link will not claim

somebody else's mineral. That refusal is deliberate.

  • Their mineral is asleep until they sign Claude in. Nothing runs before they

turn the key themselves, which is also why an invitation sitting unopened costs nobody anything.

Approving people who ask to join

If you list your rock publicly, people can ask. Requests queue for you, and you approve each one yourself: there is no setting that admits strangers automatically, and there is not going to be.

Removing someone

You can end a tie from the Members page. What that does and does not do:

  • It ends the membership: your skills stop arriving, your shared pages stop

arriving, they leave your roster.

  • It does not delete their mineral, their brain or their assistant.
  • If they were anchored, hosting stops being yours to pay for. The machine is

still theirs.

There is deliberately no host action that destroys a member's mineral. If a member wants their machine gone, they ask us and we destroy it, and that is their decision to make rather than yours.

Handing a mineral over

Custody can move in both directions, and either direction needs the sign-in of the person receiving it. A community can hand a mineral to the member on it, and a member can accept custody of one that was anchored to a community.

Nothing flips behind anyone's back. Every change to custody, every support grant and every removal is written to a log the member can read in their own app, and pushed to their Telegram.

Reading the roster

Each row shows the member's mineral, how it is tied, whether it is reachable and signed in, and which software build it is running. That last column is the one worth scanning: a member on an old build is a member missing fixes, and a mineral too old to report its build reads as "software unknown", which tells you the same thing.
