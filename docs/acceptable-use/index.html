Acceptable use

Version 0.1, effective 2026-08-24. Samuel Davis trading as Crads AI.

A mineral is a machine on the internet with your credentials on it and an assistant that can act. This page is the short list of what you must not point it at. Part of the terms of service.

Version 0.1, effective 24 August 2026.

Do not

  • Break the law with it. Any law that applies to you.
  • Use it against other people. No harassment, no stalking, no building

profiles of people who have not agreed to it, no scraping someone's data in a way they have not agreed to, no attacking anyone's systems.

  • Put other people's confidential material on it unless you are entitled to

hold that material. Your own client list, yes. A list you took from a former employer, no.

  • Send bulk unsolicited mail from it. Your assistant can reach your inbox.

That is not a mailing tool and using it as one will get your own email domain burned before we ever notice.

  • **Use it to generate material that sexualises children, incites violence, or

is designed to deceive someone into losing money.** This is a short list and it is not negotiable.

  • Resell it as your own product. Hosting minerals for other people is a

rock, and a rock has its own agreement. Ask.

  • Attack the platform. Do not try to reach other members' minerals, the

directory, or our infrastructure. If you find a way to, tell us; see below.

Some judgement, honestly

Your assistant works from your notes and can act on your accounts. Two things follow that are your call and not ours to police:

  • What you put in your brain is up to you, and we do not look. If you keep

material there that would be damaging in the wrong hands, put credentials in the sealed tier and read the privacy policy on what a support session can see.

  • Automating a decision is still your decision. If you schedule your

assistant to send things on your behalf, what it sends is from you. Read the cadence you install.

Security research

If you find a vulnerability, we want to hear it, and you will not be in trouble for having looked. Email cradsdavis@gmail.com with what you found and how.

What we ask: do not use other members' minerals as your test target, do not exfiltrate anyone's data to prove a point, and give us a chance to fix it before you publish. What you get: a real answer from the person who wrote the code, and credit if you want it.

If you break this

We will talk to you first, unless the use is unlawful, is harming someone, or is a live security problem. In those cases we act immediately and then tell you what we did and why, per section 8 of the terms.

Your data comes with you either way. We do not hold a brain hostage over a dispute about conduct.
