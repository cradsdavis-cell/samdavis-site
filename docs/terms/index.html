Terms of service

Plain terms for the beta. Between you and Samuel Davis, trading as Crads AI, ABN 26 929 349 775, Coogee NSW, Australia ("we", "us"). By opening a mineral you agree to these.

Version 0.1, effective 24 August 2026. A lawyer reviews these before the beta converts to paid; the version and date change whenever the text does.

1. What you are getting#

A mineral: a virtual machine we provision and maintain for you, in the EU, running an assistant against a brain that belongs to you. Either a pebble (one person's private workspace) or a rock (a community that hosts pebbles).

We maintain the machinery: the container image, the engine, the app, the scheduler and the system pages. You own the content: your brain, your pages, your skills, your cadence, your connected services.

2. The beta, and what it costs#

Nothing, for now. Cohort one is a free beta. It exists so the product is tested by real use, not to bill you.

  • No charge applies during the beta window.
  • Pricing shown anywhere in the app or the docs is indicative and is not an

offer. Nothing is billed and no payment method is required.

  • There is a decision point on 26 September 2026, when pricing and

continuation are settled openly. You will be told what is proposed before anything is charged, and you can decline and keep your data.

  • We will not convert you to a paid plan silently. No charge without your

explicit agreement to a stated price.

We are not registered for GST, so no GST is charged.

3. Your account and your responsibility#

  • You sign in with your own accounts. Your assistant runs on your Claude

account under your agreement with Anthropic, and your usage is subject to it.

  • Keep your sign-ins to yourself. An invitation is a door: once you have claimed

a mineral, your account holds it.

  • You are responsible for what you ask your assistant to do and for what you

connect it to. If you connect it to your business email, it can act on your business email.

  • Anything you put on your mineral must be lawful and yours to put there. See the

acceptable use policy.

4. What we promise#

  • We do not read your notes. Not as a courtesy, as a consequence of where

they sit. The privacy policy sets out the exceptions honestly, including what a support session you grant can see.

  • We tell you when something is broken, including when we caused it.
  • Every fix ships with the test that pins it. That is how this is built and

it is the reason to trust a fix rather than a reassurance.

  • You can leave with everything. Your brain is a git repository you already

hold. There is no export step and no window where we are the only copy.

  • We give notice. If we change these terms materially, stop a feature you

rely on, or wind the service down, you hear it with a date attached. What happens in a wind-down is set out in the continuity page and those commitments are part of these terms.

5. What we do not promise#

Being specific is more use to you than a disclaimer.

  • No uptime guarantee. This is a beta run by one person. Your mineral may be

unreachable and a scheduled job may not run.

  • No guarantee your assistant is right. It is a language model working from

your notes. It will sometimes be confidently wrong. Check anything that matters before you act on it, and never treat its output as legal, financial, medical or tax advice.

  • No guarantee a connected service keeps working. Google, Microsoft and the

rest change their APIs and their rules whenever they like. When one breaks we fix it if we can and tell you if we cannot.

  • No guarantee of data recovery beyond your own backup. Your mineral takes a

nightly encrypted backup to a repository you own. If you have not connected one, and the machine dies, the content that only existed there is gone. Connect the repository.

  • We are one person. Not a company with a support rota. Response times depend

on one human being awake and not on a mountain.

6. What we may do to your mineral#

  • Update it. Your mineral restarts nightly onto the software your community

has published. That changes machinery, not your files. You can switch it off, and then you are running old software with old bugs.

  • Come in if you ask us to. Support access needs a grant from you, expires on

a clock, and is logged on your own mineral. There is no standing key.

  • Suspend it, only for the reasons in section 8.

7. Ending it#

You, at any time. Ask and we destroy the machine and its disk and remove your pointers from the directory. Keep your repository, or take the box itself. We do not require a reason and there is nothing to claw back.

Us, with notice. Thirty days in writing, except for section 8. Before the lights go out on your mineral, ownership is yours outright and your backup is confirmed working.

8. When we can act immediately#

Only for: unlawful use, using your mineral to attack or harm someone, non-payment after a stated price is agreed and chased, or a live security problem where waiting is the greater harm. In each case we tell you what happened and why, and your data comes with you.

9. Liability, stated honestly#

This is a free beta from a sole trader. Our liability to you is limited to what you have paid us, which during the beta is nothing. We are not liable for lost profit, lost opportunity, or anything your assistant got wrong that you acted on without checking.

Nothing here excludes rights you have under the Australian Consumer Law that cannot be excluded. If a term of these terms is unenforceable, the rest still stands.

10. Your content, and ours#

Your brain, your pages and your data are yours. We claim no licence over them beyond what is technically required to run the machine for you, and we will not train anything on them or use them to build anything.

The engine, the app, the images and the documentation are ours. You may use them to run your mineral. Do not resell them as your own product; if you want to host minerals for other people, that is a rock, and it has its own agreement.

11. Changes#

Material changes get told to you with a date, not slipped into a page. The version and effective date at the top of this document change whenever the text does, and the previous versions stay on the record.

12. Law, and complaints#
