Connections

What your assistant can reach.

Every outside service your mineral is wired to, whether it is working, and when it last did. Read from your mineral when you open the page, so it is the current answer rather than a remembered one.

Adding one#

Sixty-two services are in the catalogue, listed in what you can connect. Most are one click: you sign in and it is done.

Google is the exception, and it is the exception on purpose. Reading your mail means either a company sits in the middle of it, or you make your own key. Crads AI chose the second, which costs you ten minutes once and means your Google key never touches our infrastructure.

Anything not in the catalogue can be added by URL, with a token, from this same page.

When one stops working#

It happens, and almost never because of something you did. A sign-in reaches the end of its life, or a service changes a rule.

The row says which, in plain words, and asks you to sign in again if that is the fix. It does not retry forever and it does not ping you hourly: one clear statement, in the place you would look. The walkthrough is fix a connection that has stopped working.

Why this page matters more than it looks#

Your scheduled jobs read through these connections while you are asleep. A job that needed a dead connection fails rather than pretending: it does not send a half-empty brief and does not quietly skip the part it could not read.

So when a morning brief does not arrive, this page is the first place to look, before concluding your assistant has stopped working.
