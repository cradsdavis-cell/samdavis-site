Explained

How updates reach you

Software arrives on your mineral by itself. Worth understanding, because it is the one thing that changes your machine without you asking.

What happens

Once a night your mineral restarts onto the software your community has published for it. The restart takes about a minute. Every box in a fleet gets its own slightly different time, so a hundred minerals do not all restart on the same second.

What that changes: the machinery. The engine, the app, the scheduler, the system pages.

What it does not touch: your brain, your pages, your skills, your cadence, your connected services, your files. All of it is exactly as you left it.

If you were told an update is out and you do not want to wait for tonight, there is a button. Same restart, same minute, same nothing-of-yours-moves.

Knowing what you are running

The app shows the build your mineral is on. This matters more than it sounds: until late August 2026 a mineral could not report which engine it ran, so the answer to "have you got the fix?" was guesswork for everybody including us. Now the Members page shows it per mineral, and a mineral on software too old to say reads as "software unknown", which is itself the answer.

When a fix lands that you reported, the version notes say so in plain words. That is the loop closing: you told us something was broken, and the app tells you when it stopped being broken.

Turning it off

You can. There is a setting on your mineral that stops the nightly restart.

The honest consequence: you keep the software you have, including its bugs, and you stop receiving fixes for the connectors that break when Google or Microsoft change something on their end. That is a reasonable trade for a week while you finish something, and a bad one as a permanent state.

What we will not do

We do not push software to your mineral outside this channel, and we do not use the update path to look at anything. An update is a restart onto a published image; it is not a door.

The one way anybody reaches your mineral other than through the software it already has is a support session you granted, and that is set out in the privacy policy.
