Walkthrough

Your first hour

This walks the whole way in, from a machine with nothing on it to an assistant that answers you. It takes about an hour, most of which is the interview at the end, and you can stop after step 3 and come back.

You need the Crads AI app, and the email address your invitation went to.

Here is the whole shape before the detail. Your mineral already exists by the time you meet it, and it does nothing at all until you claim it:

1. Open the door#

The first screen is the door. It asks which mineral you are opening.

On a machine that has never opened one, the list is empty and the door offers to claim your invitation instead.

Click your invitation, sign in with the account it was sent to, and the door hands you the mineral. Two things are worth knowing about that sign-in:

  • The invitation is only a door. Once you have signed in, your account holds

the mineral, and the link stops being the thing that grants access.

  • It has to be the address the invitation went to. A forwarded link will not

claim someone else's mineral, by design.

2. Sign Claude in#

Your mineral is inert until you sign Claude in on it. This is deliberate: it is your assistant, running on your box, using your Claude account.

Follow the prompt, approve the code, and come back. Nothing else in this tutorial works until this step is done. The other two arming steps are your first scheduled task and connecting Telegram, which is where briefs and anything needing your yes will reach you.

3. Look around an empty mineral#

Now the app opens on your mineral. It looks sparse, because it is: nothing knows anything about you yet.

The sidebar is worth naming now, because the rest of the documentation assumes it. Two of these are single pages and three are groups you open:

  • Overview is home: *what your assistant knows and how

it is doing*. The Health card here is also where the jobs your mineral runs by itself report in.

  • Your pebble is this mineral itself: *who owns it,

where its backup goes, and anything waiting on your answer*. Your pebble is your own private workspace, and nobody else can read into it.

  • Network opens onto Map, *the rocks above your mineral

and the pebbles under it*, and Organisations, the communities you are tied to. It is a picture of how your mineral is connected, not a list of people you know.

  • Your assistant opens onto Brain (*everything your

assistant knows), Skills (what your assistant can do, and when it runs) and Connections (what your assistant can reach*).

  • Privacy and access opens onto Secrets and

Sharing: what leaves your mineral, and who receives it. The short answer is almost nothing, and only what you turn on.

  • Terminal sits on its own at the bottom. That is where

you talk to your assistant directly.

4. Do the interview#

The last step is the long one. Your assistant interviews you, and out of that interview it builds your brain: who you are, what you are trying to do, who is in your world, how you work, what to never touch.

Start it by opening the Terminal tab and asking for it:

/onboard

It runs as a conversation, one question at a time, and it is resumable. Nobody answers all of it in one sitting, and it is designed for you not to. Leave it and come back; it picks up where it stopped.

When it has enough, it writes your brain as a set of linked pages you own and can read. That is the thing everything else reads from.
