How to

Machines that can open your mineral

Your mineral keeps a roster of the machines allowed to open it, and the roster is the only door: a key that is not on it does not work, and removing a key from it genuinely shuts that machine out.

The list lives below the map on Map, and anything that changes it is written to a log you can read and pushed to your Telegram.

Letting a new computer in#

On the new machine, the app offers Let this computer in. What happens next is a handshake with one human step in it:

  1. The new machine shows a six-character safety code.
  2. The same code appears where you approve it.
  3. You check they match before you approve.

The code is derived from the machine's actual key, so a matching code means you are letting in the machine in front of you, not one pretending to be it. Nobody at Crads AI can approve a machine onto your mineral; the approval is yours.

Removing one#

Remove a machine from the roster and its key stops opening your mineral. Not eventually: the roster is what the door reads.

One guard exists for your own protection. Removing the only working key would lock you out of your own mineral permanently (there is no back door to fall back on, by design), so a removal that would empty the roster is refused with an explanation instead of being obeyed.

Every removal is stamped and logged, and your Telegram hears about it.

The key your mineral may be born with#

If a community had your mineral built for you, it arrives with one row already on the roster: "Crads-AI setup access (not your computer)". That is our setup key, named as ours, because somebody had to build the machine before you could reach it.

It is removed at handover and the removal is checked. You can also remove it yourself, at any time, without asking. The full story, including what it means while it is there, is in the privacy policy.

Renaming#

Machines can be renamed so the roster reads as "my laptop" and "the studio Mac" rather than as key fingerprints. Renaming changes the label only; keys and access are untouched.
