Every page of the app

Organisations

The rocks your mineral is tied to, and the rocks that have opened their door.

Two lists on one page: the communities you are in, and the communities you could ask to join.

The communities you are in#

Each tie shows which rock, and how you are tied to it: joined if you brought your own mineral, anchored if that community had it built for you and pays for its hosting.

You can leave from here. Leaving ends what the community sends you: its published skills stop arriving, its shared pages stop arriving, you come off its roster. Leaving does not touch your mineral, your brain or your assistant, including when you were anchored. The arrangement ends; the machine is still yours.

The communities you could join#

A rock is private by default: people join by invitation only. A rock that has chosen to list publicly appears here, and you can ask to join it.

Asking is not joining. The request queues for that community's host, who approves each one by hand. There is no setting anywhere that admits strangers automatically.

What joining a community gives them#

Not your brain. A rock receives the status record described in what your community can see, which carries no page, note, message or name from your mineral, and two switches on your Sharing page control most of even that.

What it gives you is the community's curation: the skills they publish, the pages they share, and somebody to tell when something is wrong.
