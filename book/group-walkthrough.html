Group walkthrough

The walkthrough, run for up to three people at once. Each of you sets up your own assistant; the sessions are shared.

  • 1½ hours + 30 min
  • Up to 3 people
  • A$250 a seat
Book a seat ↓

  1. Before session 1

    You install the app and Claude Code and create your own mineral. The docs walk you through it and I'm on Slack if you get stuck. Please arrive with it done: in a group, one setup still installing holds up the other two.

  2. Session 1, an hour and a half

    Group video call, screen shared: how to actually use Claude Code and the Crads-AI app. You leave with one piece of homework.

  3. Homework, about an hour

    The onboarding interview, on your own. It writes the first version of your brain from your answers.

  4. Follow-up, 30 minutes

    With your group, booked afterwards, at least a day later: we look at what each brain learned, go through the CRIT prompting method, and take your questions. You keep written docs of your setup.

Support and what to bring

From session 1 you get your first month of membership: Slack support for questions about your own setup, answered within a working day, and the weekly group drop-in call. After that you can stay on as a member, or leave it there; the assistant stays yours either way. Not included: the server bill, paid to your hosting provider, and your Claude subscription. Sessions are not recorded.

Bring: a laptop with the app and Claude Code installed, a Claude subscription, and your own mineral, on a small server in your own hosting account.
