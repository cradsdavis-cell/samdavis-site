Group walkthrough

1½ hours + 30 min, in a group of up to 3 · A$250 a seat · email me to book a seat.

The walkthrough, run for up to three people at once. Each of you sets up your own assistant; the sessions are shared.

Before session 1, you install the app and Claude Code and create your own mineral. The docs walk you through it and I'm on Slack if you get stuck. Please arrive with it done: in a group, one setup still installing holds up the other two.

Session 1 (an hour and a half, group video call, screen shared): how to actually use Claude Code and the Crads-AI app. You leave with one piece of homework.

Homework, about an hour on your own: the onboarding interview. It writes the first version of your brain from your answers.

Follow-up (30 min, with your group, booked afterwards, at least a day later): we look at what each brain learned, go through the CRIT prompting method, and take your questions. You keep written docs of your setup.

From session 1 you get your first month of membership: Slack support for questions about your own setup, answered within a working day, and the weekly group drop-in call. After that you can stay on as a member, or leave it there; the assistant stays yours either way. Not included: the server bill, paid to your hosting provider, and your Claude subscription. Sessions are not recorded.

Bring: a laptop with the app and Claude Code installed, a Claude subscription, and your own mineral, on a small server in your own hosting account.

Groups are put together by email, so everyone in a session is at a similar stage. Send me a line about your business and whether you've installed the app yet, and I'll come back with a time and a link to book and pay for your seat.
