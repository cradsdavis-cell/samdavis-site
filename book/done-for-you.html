Done for you

Built and working. Your part: one form, two calls.

  • 1 hour + 30 min
  • A$1,500
  • First month of membership
Pick a time ↓

You end with

  • Installed on your own server, connected to Claude and backed up to your GitHub, all by me.
  • It knows your business, written in from your form.
  • Your email, calendar and the other tools you work in, like Notion or Google Drive, connected on limits you choose.
  • Two skills for jobs you repeat every week, picked on the call, and a morning brief.
  • A handover showing you how to use it day to day.
  • Written docs, and your first month of membership.

  1. Before the call: a form

    The link comes in your welcome email. Who you work with, how you work, how you sound, and the jobs you'd most like off your plate. It's what I build from.

  2. The call, 60 minutes

    Video call. We go through your answers and your tools, settle what it should and shouldn't touch, and pick the two jobs it takes on first.

  3. I build it, over about a week

    On a small server in your own hosting account. You let me in from the app with one tap, for as long as the build takes, and you can close that access any time. It learns your business from the form and from your tools, I connect your email, calendar and the other tools you work in, like Notion or Google Drive, build two skills for jobs you do every week, and switch on a morning brief.

  4. The handover, 30 minutes

    You book it afterwards, at least a week after the call. I show you how to use it day to day, and you keep written docs of your setup.

Support and what to bring

From the call your first month of membership is included: answers on Slack about your own setup within a working day, and the weekly group drop-in call. After that, stay on at A$99 a month, or leave it there; the assistant stays yours either way. Not included: the server bill, from about €6 a month paid to your host, and your Claude subscription.

Bring: a Claude subscription (Pro or above), a GitHub account and a Hetzner account. If you don't have them yet, we make them together on the call.
