Guided setup

Two 1-hour sessions · A$700 · pick your first slot below.

Session 1 (60 min, video call, screen shared): we build your server in your own hosting account, connect your GitHub backup, sign Claude in and get the app running on your machine, with Telegram if you want it. You leave with a short homework list.

Homework, about an hour on your own: the onboarding interview. It writes the first version of the brain from your answers.

Session 2 (60 min, you book it afterwards, at least a day later): we read what the interview produced together, switch on the built-in schedules your week needs (morning brief, inbox triage, weekly review) and start the voice loop. You keep a written record of the setup.

Between sessions a question by message is fine. Not included: the hosting bill (about €4 to €30 a month at Hetzner, US$24 to $96 at DigitalOcean) and your Claude subscription. Sessions are not recorded. Done means it runs without me.

Bring: a laptop, a GitHub account, a Claude subscription, and a Hetzner or DigitalOcean account (or twenty minutes to make one).

Pay once for both sessions. After payment you get a calendar invite for session 1, a receipt, and a sign-in to book session 2. 24h cancellation policy.
