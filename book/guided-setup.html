The same first hour, then two hours wiring it into your email, calendar and Slack or WhatsApp, with your first skills and schedules.

Pick a time ↓

  1. Session 1, 60 minutes

    Video call, screen shared: how to actually use Claude Code and the Crads-AI app, and we build your server in your own hosting account (from about €6 a month, billed to you by the host), so it runs while you sleep and you can talk to it from the Claude app on your phone, with your morning brief on Telegram. You leave with one piece of homework.

  2. Homework, about an hour

    The onboarding interview, on your own. It writes the first version of the brain from your answers.

  3. Session 2, 120 minutes

    You book it afterwards, at least a day later. I connect your email, calendar and Slack or WhatsApp, build your first skills around the work you actually do, and switch on a morning brief and your first scheduled job. You keep written docs of your setup.

Support and what to bring

From session 1 your first month of membership is included: answers on Slack about your own setup within a working day, and the weekly group drop-in call. After that, stay on at A$99 a month, or leave it there; the assistant stays yours either way. One-off help is A$233 an hour. Not included: the server bill, paid to your hosting provider, and your Claude subscription. Sessions are not recorded. By the end your business has an assistant that runs without you.

Bring: a laptop, a GitHub account, a Claude subscription, and a Hetzner or DigitalOcean account (or twenty minutes to make one), or a server you already have. We settle where it lives in session 1.
