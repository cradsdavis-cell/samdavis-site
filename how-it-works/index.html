How it works

It is not a chatbot. It is a system that accumulates you.

Here is what a Crads-AI assistant is actually made of, four layers, and how the app builds them with you through the interview and daily use.

What it is made of

A chatbot forgets you. An assistant accumulates you.

The difference is a folder of plain files it reads every time it wakes up. That folder is what the app builds with you: four layers, each one making the assistant more yours. This page is about how yours gets built; the docs walk every part of a finished one.

C1

Context

What it knows about you.

Plain files, organised in layers. Mine has eight:

1North Star : why
2Philosophy : how you think
3Self : who you are
4Network : your people
5Past : where you've been
6Goals : where you're going
7Tasks : what's on now
8Workflow : how your week runs

Written down once, never explained twice.

C2

Connections

What it can reach.

Context tells it who you are. Connections let it work where the work already lives:

EmailCalendarTasks WhatsAppDocs & DriveCRM / books

Each one is a doorway you open once.

C3

Capabilities

Blueprints for your workflows.

A capability is a written recipe: the steps you'd give a new hire, saved so it runs the same way every time:

Morning briefInbox sweep Client debriefWeekly review

Describe the workflow once. Now it's a button.

C4

Cadence

The heartbeat.

Jobs that run on a schedule without being asked: the morning brief is waiting before you sit down.

You've hired an executive assistant. Give it feedback, and let go of the reins a little more each week.

The app climbs this ladder with you: Context first (the interview), then the doorways, then the blueprints, then the heartbeat.

Where it lives

Three places, all yours. Nothing sits with Crads-AI, because there is nothing central to sit with.

  • The app, on your laptop. Windows or Mac. It builds the server, then it is how you open and steer your assistant every day.
  • The mineral, on your server. A small machine in your own hosting account, built by the app with your own API token, holding only your computer's key. This is where the assistant runs and where its scheduled jobs happen while you sleep.
  • The brain, on your GitHub. Everything it knows about you, as plain files in a private repository you own, pushed there every night. Lose the server and you lose nothing.

What you have once it is running

The app on your laptop, your assistant (we call it a mineral) on a server you own, and a brain repo on your GitHub that holds everything it knows about you. Your tools connected, a couple of workflows you actually use, and the method to keep building. It is not "finished" on day one; roughly a third of it is built, and the rest compounds through daily use over the months that follow. The docs walk each part.
