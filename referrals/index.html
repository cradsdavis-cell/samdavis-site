Referrals

If you send someone my way and they buy a Guided Setup, they get A$100 off, and I pay you A$100 once their first session has happened.

Your link remembers you for 90 days

You get your own link, like crads-ai.com/r/your-name. When someone opens it, their browser remembers that you sent them, for 90 days.

Most people book the free AI roadmap call first and decide on a Guided Setup afterwards. That still counts: if they buy within the 90 days, the referral is yours. If two people send the same person, the first link they opened counts.

A$100 for you, A$100 off for them

  • A$100 for you when someone you sent buys a Guided Setup.
  • A$100 off for them: the Guided Setup is A$600 instead of A$700 when they book through your link. The booking page shows it and it comes off at checkout. There's no code to pass on, only the link.
  • Their discount doesn't combine with other discount codes. If they use a different code, you're still paid.
  • The free roadmap call pays nothing on its own. It's usually the step before a Guided Setup, which is why the 90 days carry through it.

Paid after their first session, once a month

The A$100 is owed once their first session has happened. I pay what's owed once a month, by bank transfer. Before the first payment I'll ask for your bank details, and your ABN if you have one.

If they didn't use your link

The checkout asks "Who sent you?". If they put your name there, or mention you when they book, I'll match it up by hand and you're still paid. Their A$100 off only comes through the link, so send the link rather than just my name.

Please say it's paid

When you recommend Crads-AI, please mention that you get a referral fee. One line is enough, for example: "Book through my link and you get A$100 off a Guided Setup, and I get A$100 too." People should know when a recommendation is paid.
