Referrals

If you send someone my way and they buy a Guided Setup, I pay you A$100 once their first session has happened.

Your link remembers you for 90 days

You get your own link, like crads-ai.com/r/your-name. When someone opens it, their browser remembers that you sent them, for 90 days.

Most people book the free AI roadmap call first and decide on a Guided Setup afterwards. That still counts: if they buy within the 90 days, the referral is yours. If two people send the same person, the first link they opened counts.

A$100 for each Guided Setup

  • A$100 when someone you sent buys a Guided Setup (A$700).
  • The free roadmap call pays nothing on its own. It's usually the step before a Guided Setup, which is why the 90 days carry through it.
  • The person you send pays the normal price. There's no discount code to pass on.

Paid after their first session, once a month

The A$100 is owed once their first session has happened. I pay what's owed once a month, by bank transfer. Before the first payment I'll ask for your bank details, and your ABN if you have one.

If they didn't use your link

The checkout asks "Who sent you?". If they put your name there, or mention you when they book, I'll match it up by hand.

Please say it's paid

When you recommend Crads-AI, please mention that you get a referral fee. One line is enough, for example: "I get A$100 if you buy a Guided Setup through my link." People should know when a recommendation is paid.
