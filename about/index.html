Hi, I'm Sam.

I set up AI assistants for people who run small businesses and would rather not become technical to get one. The assistant learns how you work: it drafts in your voice, keeps track of your clients and tells you what's slipping before it drops. The people I've set it up for say it has given them hours of their week back.

I trained as a scientist first. A first-class degree in maths and physics at Manchester. A summer at the European Space Agency at 22. A PhD in Bayesian statistics at the University of Sydney. Somewhere along the way I noticed I kept ending up in the same job, which was taking something technical and making it work for the people in the room.

I'm Welsh (Caradog is my middle name), I grew up in Eryri, and Sydney has been home since 2022.

Every job I've had has been the same job

European Space Agency, 2019. At 22 I was handed the propellant budget for an Earth-observation satellite. I built a probability-based alternative to the worst-case method the team used, and they asked me to write it up as a paper.

Alan Turing Institute, 2022. Five days with ten data scientists and a problem from Cefas, the UK government's fisheries science agency. I facilitated; by Friday we had a working pipeline and presented it to their marine team.

University of Sydney, 2023 to 2025. Three years teaching second-year engineering mechanics. The course coordinator asked me to write the full exam.

Small businesses, 2025 to now. Eight founders through The Impact Collab's Expert Pod, then seven paying clients from early 2026. Same job again: a powerful tool, made to fit how one person actually runs their week.

You can check my work

The assistant I set up is called Crads-AI. I built it, I run my own week on it, and in September I made it free and open source, so every line of it is public on GitHub. It runs on your own server or your own computer, under your own accounts. How it works, if you're curious.
