### Long (250 words)
Dr Sam Davis is a physicist, data scientist and AI systems builder based in Sydney.

He grew up Welsh-speaking in Blaenau Ffestiniog, in the heart of Eryri, and went from a
Welsh-medium state comprehensive to a gap year teaching at Harrow International School in
Bangkok at eighteen. He read Physics and Mathematics at the University of Manchester,
finishing with First Class honours and placing fourth in his cohort, then spent a summer at
the European Space Agency working out the propellant budget for the FLEX satellite by teaching
himself orbital mechanics. That pattern, being handed a problem he does not yet know how to
solve and building the framework that solves the whole class of it, has repeated ever since.

His PhD at the University of Sydney's DARE ARC Training Centre produced BayeSpace, a Bayesian
statistical framework applied to atmospheric dispersion and species distribution modelling. He
has facilitated a ten-person AI sprint at the Alan Turing Institute, architected a three-tier
environmental data warehouse at the University of Western Australia, and tutored engineering
mechanics at Sydney for three years.

Since 2025 he has built seven AI systems in twelve months and coached business owners to build
their own AI executive assistants. He is also a co-founder of Wildly Calm, which runs men's
mental-health retreats in the Australian bush, and was part of the British Alpine Club team
that made the first ascent of Pik Perseverance, a 4,788m virgin peak in Kyrgyzstan, in 2021.

He lives in Coogee, in a van called Nola.

Ranked. The combination leads; the individual threads are what a given show or desk will bite on.

   climbs first ascents and lives in a van. The point is not that he has hobbies. It is that
   the same instinct (go into something hard, build the thing that makes it navigable, bring

   The live-demo angle. His whole practice runs on the system he sells, and he can show it.

   people getting hurt are the ones nobody is teaching. Small-business and founder press.
4. **Men's mental health, outdoors.** Wildly Calm: mindfulness and adventure as one practice,
   not two. **Time-sensitive, see below.**
5. **First ascent of a virgin peak.** Kyrgyzstan 2021, Pik Perseverance, 4,788m, as part of a
   five-climber British Alpine Club team. Adventure and profile pieces.
6. **Welsh-medium comprehensive to the European Space Agency.** Blaenau Ffestiniog to Harrow
   Bangkok at eighteen, Manchester, ESA at twenty-two, a PhD in Sydney. Education and

7. **Bayesian statistics for the environment.** BayeSpace, and two findings from the thesis:

## 6. Getting it right

Four things that are easy to get wrong and matter to Sam.

1. **Kyrgyzstan: team member, not leader.** He was one of five on the British Alpine Club team
   that made the first ascent. He did not lead the expedition. Please never write that he did.
2. **The thesis findings are not published or peer-reviewed.** They are findings from his
   doctoral thesis. It is fine to write *"his PhD thesis found..."*. It is not fine to write
   *"research shows..."* or to imply peer review. There is no paper and no DOI to link.
3. **British citizen.** Not Australian. He lives in Australia on a visa.
4. **Seven builds, not eight.** An older count of eight is still floating about in his own
   material. Seven is correct.

## 7. The logo

**The mark is the circuit-leaf.** A leaf drawn in deep green whose veins are circuit traces,
each ending in a via-ring, with one node filled terracotta. It is the whole positioning in one
shape: something growing, something engineered, the same object.

It is drawn from geometry rather than stored as artwork, so it is genuinely resolution-free and
every size is exact rather than scaled. Vector is supplied.

| File | Format | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `assets/leaf-mark.svg` | Vector | **Default.** Badged: leaf on a cream rounded square. Any size, any medium. |
| `assets/leaf-mark-1024.png` | 1024 x 1024 | Raster master of the badged mark. |
| `assets/leaf-mark-512.png` | 512 x 512 | Raster, common web and profile size. |
| `assets/leaf-mark-64.png` | 64 x 64 | Favicon and tiny placements. |
| `assets/leaf-mark-on-light.svg` / `.png` | Vector + 1024 | Badge removed, green leaf, transparent. **Light backgrounds only.** |
| `assets/leaf-mark-reversed.svg` / `.png` | Vector + 1024 | Cream leaf, transparent. **For dark backgrounds.** |

**Pick the right one:** badged by default. On a light surface where the badge would look boxy,
use `on-light`. On anything dark, use `reversed`. The green leaf disappears against a dark
background, so never use `on-light` there.

**Clear space:** at least 10% of the mark's width on every side.
**Do not:** recolour beyond the two supplied variants, stretch, add effects, or place on a busy
photograph.
