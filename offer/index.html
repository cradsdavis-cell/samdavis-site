Walkthrough

1 hour + 30 min · 1:1 · screen shared · at least a day apart

A$350

Fixed. Pay once for both sessions.

You leave with

  • CRIT, the method for briefing it, so you can solve the next one yourself.

How it runs

  • Before session 1: you install the app and set up your assistant. The first-hour guide walks you through it, and session 1 finishes it if you get stuck.

  • Homework, about an hour: the onboarding interview, which writes the first version of what it knows about you.
  • Session 2 (30 min, a day or more later): we fix the gaps and solve one real problem with it.

By the end you can solve your own business problems with Claude.

Guided setup

1 hour + 2 hours · 1:1 · screen shared · at least a day apart

A$700

Fixed. Pay once for both sessions.

You leave with

  • Everything in the walkthrough.
  • Your email, calendar and Slack or WhatsApp connected, on limits you choose (drafts only and never sends, if that's what you want).
  • Your first skills, built around the jobs you repeat every week: follow-ups, proposals, posts, notes.
  • A morning brief that reads your day and tells you what matters, and jobs that run on a schedule.
  • Written docs, and your first month of membership: answers on Slack within a working day, and the weekly drop-in call.

How it runs

  • Session 1 (60 min): we build your server in your own hosting account, and I show you the app.
  • Homework, about an hour: the onboarding interview.
  • Session 2 (120 min, a day or more later): I connect your tools, build your first skills around the work you actually do, and switch on the morning brief.

By the end your business has an assistant that runs without you.
