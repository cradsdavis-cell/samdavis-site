Setup and support

The software is free. My time is not.

Crads-AI is free, open source and self-hosted; the docs get most people there on their own. When you would rather have a person in the room, this is what that costs.

Which one is you?

  • "I want it set up properly, with someone who has done it before." Guided setup.
  • "It is running. I have one question." Working session.
  • "I want to be able to ask again after that." Hourly support, on request.
Where most people start

Guided setup

2 × 60 min · 1:1 · video call · at least a day apart

A$700

Fixed. Pay once for both sessions.

  • Session 1 (60 min, screen shared): your server built in your own hosting account, GitHub backup connected, Claude signed in, the app running on your machine, Telegram if you want it. You leave with a short homework list.
  • Homework, about an hour: the onboarding interview. It writes the first version of the brain from your answers.
  • Session 2 (60 min, at least a day later): we read what the interview produced together, switch on the built-in schedules your week needs (morning brief, inbox triage, weekly review) and start the voice loop.
  • Written docs of your setup, yours to keep. A question by message between the sessions is fine.

Not included: the hosting bill (about €4 to €30 a month at Hetzner, US$24 to $96 at DigitalOcean) and your Claude subscription. Sessions are not recorded. Support after that is by the hour, on request. Done means it runs without me.
