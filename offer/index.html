What you end with, and how it runs

You end with

  • Crads-AI installed on your own server or computer and connected to your Claude account, together in session 1.

  • Session 2 (30 min, a day or more later): we check what it learned, fix the gaps, and back it all up to your own private GitHub.

Where it lives. On a small server in your own hosting account, from about €6 a month, so it runs while you sleep and you can talk to it from the Claude app on your phone. Not included: the server bill, paid to your host, and your Claude subscription. Setup sessions are not recorded.

Or it can run on Claude's own cloud, inside your Claude plan, with no server bill.
